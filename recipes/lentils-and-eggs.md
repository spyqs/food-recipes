## Steps

1. Warm the lentils and put them on a plate.
2. Heat the oil in a pan over medium heat and fry the eggs for 3 to 4 minutes, until done how you like them.
3. Slide the eggs over the lentils and season with salt and black pepper.

## Prep ahead

Cook the lentils in a batch, or use a precooked pouch and skip this.
