## Steps

1. Lower the eggs into a pot of boiling water and cook for 10 minutes.
2. Cool under cold water and peel.
3. Mash the eggs with a fork, then mix in the rinsed beans and mayonnaise. Season with salt and black pepper.

## Prep ahead

Boil a batch of eggs and keep them in the fridge, in their shells, for up to a week. This turns lunch into a 3-minute job.

## Notes

Mustard, paprika or dill are good additions.
