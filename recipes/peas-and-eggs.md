## Steps

1. Heat the oil in a pan over medium heat and cook the peas from frozen for 3 to 4 minutes, until hot and any water has cooked off.
2. Crack in the eggs and stir for 1 to 2 minutes, until just set.
3. Season with salt and black pepper.

## Notes

Chili flakes or a squeeze of lemon are good additions.
