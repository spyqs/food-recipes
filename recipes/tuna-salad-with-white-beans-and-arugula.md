## Steps

1. Drain the tuna and rinse the beans.
2. Mix the tuna, beans and mayonnaise in a bowl. Season with salt and black pepper.
3. Pile the microgreens on top, or fold them in just before eating.

## Notes

No cooking at all, so this is the fastest lunch in the menu. Safe Catch cans are packed without added water, so a drained can is close to the full 5 oz of fish, which is why the protein is higher than regular canned tuna. Lemon juice or a splash of olive oil over the greens is good. A second tablespoon of mayonnaise adds about 95 calories and 10 g fat. Rinsing the beans and using the no-salt-added tuna keeps sodium moderate.
