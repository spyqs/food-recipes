## Steps

1. Lower the eggs into a pot of boiling water and cook for 10 minutes, adding the frozen peas for the last 3 minutes.
2. Drain, then cool everything under cold water and peel the eggs.
3. Mash the eggs with a fork, then mix in the peas and mayonnaise. Season with salt and black pepper.

## Notes

Mustard, paprika or dill are good additions.
