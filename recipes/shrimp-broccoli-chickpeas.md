## Steps

1. Toss the frozen broccoli and rinsed chickpeas with the oil, garlic powder, paprika and salt. Air fry at 400°F for 5 minutes.
2. Add the frozen shrimp and air fry for 10 more minutes, shaking halfway.
3. Check that the shrimp are pink and opaque all the way through, then plate.

## Notes

Pat the chickpeas dry after rinsing so they crisp up. A squeeze of lemon is a good addition.
