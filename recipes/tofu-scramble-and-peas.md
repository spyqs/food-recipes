## Steps

1. Crumble the tofu by hand into a nonstick pan and cook over medium-high heat for 5 minutes, until the water has cooked off and it starts to brown.
2. Add the frozen peas and cook for 3 to 4 minutes, until hot.
3. Stir in the soy sauce and season with black pepper.

## Notes

Garlic powder or chili flakes are good additions.
