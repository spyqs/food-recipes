## Steps

1. Pat the tofu dry, cut it into cubes and toss with the soy sauce.
2. Air fry the tofu at 400°F for 3 minutes.
3. Add the frozen broccoli and air fry for 12 more minutes, shaking halfway.

## Notes

Pressing the tofu first makes it crispier, but it's optional.
